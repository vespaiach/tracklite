Tracklite R1 — other states

States you can’t reach by clicking in Tracklite prototype: loading, load failures, server errors and changes made by someone else in the meantime. Each frame is the prototype held in that state. To see every save fail, turn on failSaves in the prototype’s Tweaks.

Missing from the kit

  • Loading indicator and a busy state for Button. Drawn as “Loading…” text and disabled buttons with “…ing” labels.
  • Toast with no dismiss control and a 5 s timeout. The kit’s Toasts always shows × and times out at 4 s.
  • Overrides applied in the prototype until the kit changes: the urgent plate and the In Progress ring use accent-2-700 so they reach 3:1 on paper; the New issue title placeholder uses ink-2; board columns share the width instead of a fixed 268 px; card handle and ⋯ show on hover or focus; the current project page is underlined.
  • Form-level message for errors not tied to one field. Drawn with FieldError above the submit button.
  • Field help text, read-only field and an inline “Couldn’t load this.” row with Retry.
  • Label colour tokens and swatches. Labels use the neutral pill with the colour name beside them.
  • BoardCard with a drag handle, ⋯ slot and title link; a column-head action slot; Pop submenus; a keyboard-drag state.
  • Tooltip that keyboard users can reach. Exact dates show through the native title on hover.
