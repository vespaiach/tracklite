Notes and gaps

  • Proposed name-saved confirmation: “Name saved.” beside Save, plus the heading and sidebar showing the new name. No toast, since toasts are for failures here.
  • Inline success / status line. Not in the kit; drawn as 12px ink-2 text with role=status for “Name saved.” and “Password changed…”.
  • Read-only field (username, email). Not in the kit; drawn as a two-column list on hairline rules rather than a disabled input.
  • Toast without a dismiss control and with a 5 s timeout; form-level error (FieldError used above the button for the rate limit); loading indicator and Button busy state; field help text. Same gaps as the signed-out screens.
  • Sidebar member menu: the kit’s Pop is used, but RailItem has no menu-button variant. Admin-only rail items (+, Members) are shown because Sam is an admin.
