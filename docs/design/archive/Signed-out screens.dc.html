Not in the kit

  • Form-level message. Drawn here with FieldError placed above the submit button; the kit has no alert for errors not tied to one field.
  • Toast without a dismiss control. The kit’s Toasts always renders ×, and useToasts times out at 4 s; STD-9 needs no × and 5 s. Drawn with the toast classes minus the button.
  • Loading indicator / spinner and a busy state for Button. Drawn as disabled buttons with “Signing in…” / “Sending…” and a “Checking your link…” line.
  • Field help text. Drawn with field-label type size in ink-2 under the input.
  • Signed-out page layout (brand bar + centred form column).
